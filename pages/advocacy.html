Harvard

The Healthy Aging Initiative.

In 2024 I founded the Harvard Chan Healthy Aging Initiative, which I direct, to bring the biology of aging together with public health across the School. It has convened the inaugural Healthy Aging Symposium, From Cells to Society: The Science of Extending Healthspan (October 2026, with Danone Institute North America), and Aging Reframed, an interdisciplinary aging seminar (2026).

Ideas

What I want everyone to understand about aging.

All six ideas
01

Life expectancy is not the rate of aging

How long we live and how fast our bodies age are different things, and biological aging is now measurable.

 02

Public health’s success gave us aging

We added decades by reducing early-life deaths, so more of us now live past the point our bodies can maintain function.

 03

Disease in old age is different

Cure one disease in an older person and you leave them with one fewer comorbidity, not health.

 04

Your ZIP code shapes how you age

Where you are born influences how fast your body ages, and when you develop chronic disease.

 05

What we can do now, and what is coming

Today’s prevention and tomorrow’s geroscience are not in competition. They inform each other.

 06

Cutting through the longevity noise

No biohacking, no false claims: what is feasible now, and what may be in the future.
The ZIP code you are born into shapes how fast your body ages.

Healthy aging has to reach every community. That makes it a public-health emergency, not a luxury problem.
